Service guide

SEO & Content

Help visitors and search engines understand what each page is about, who it helps, and where to go next.

What this service covers

Useful search content answers real questions instead of repeating keywords. This service outline focuses on reviewing existing pages, identifying gaps in service explanations, and planning content around relevant audience needs. It suits websites with unclear titles, overlapping pages, or important information hidden in short, generic descriptions.

  • An inventory of priority pages and their intended search purpose.
  • Recommendations for titles, descriptions, headings, and page structure.
  • Content briefs or revisions for agreed service pages.
  • An internal-link plan that connects related topics with descriptive labels.

How the work is organised

  1. Review the audience, service definitions, and available search evidence.
  2. Assign a distinct purpose to each priority page.
  3. Draft useful explanations and check factual claims with the content owner.
  4. Publish agreed changes and record what should be reviewed next.

Search visibility changes over time and depends on competition, technical access, and other factors. Rankings, traffic, and lead volumes are not guaranteed. Technical implementation beyond agreed content changes needs a separate scope.

Prepare your brief

Bring your page list, service descriptions, recurring customer questions, and any search reports you can lawfully share. Identify an expert who can verify the claims and explain which audience matters most.

Use Digital Marketing when an agreed offer also needs a focused campaign and measurement plan.
