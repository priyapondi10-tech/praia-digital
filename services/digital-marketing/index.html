Service guide

Digital Marketing

Connect a specific offer with a defined audience, a useful landing page, and a plan for learning what works.

What this service covers

A campaign needs more than activity across many channels. This service outline starts with one goal and a realistic view of the audience, the message, and the destination page. It suits a focused launch or a campaign that needs clearer priorities before more time or budget is committed.

  • A campaign brief covering audience, offer, and intended action.
  • A channel recommendation and an initial content or creative plan.
  • A review of landing-page message consistency.
  • A measurement plan with agreed signals and review questions.

How the work is organised

  1. Clarify the campaign goal, available assets, and operating constraints.
  2. Choose a manageable channel mix and align the destination page.
  3. Agree budgets, approval responsibilities, and measurement requirements.
  4. Review evidence and prioritise the next experiment.

Advertising spend, platform fees, creative production, and ongoing management must be separately agreed. No campaign, tracking script, advertising account, or purchase is activated by this website. Performance outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Prepare your brief

Bring the offer, audience, landing-page address, existing assets, timing constraints, and an approved budget range. Explain what a meaningful outcome looks like and who can approve messages before publication.
