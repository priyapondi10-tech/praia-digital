Service guide

Website Care

Keep a live website understandable and maintainable through routine checks, accurate updates, and a documented change process.

What this service covers

A website continues to change after launch. Service details become outdated, links move, and small issues accumulate when nobody owns the review process. This service outline focuses on a practical care routine for an existing website, with responsibilities matched to its platform and the access available.

  • A recurring checklist for important pages and navigation paths.
  • Agreed content corrections and small maintenance changes.
  • Checks of deployment status and relevant platform updates.
  • A prioritised backlog for work outside the routine care scope.

How the work is organised

  1. Inventory the platform, hosting, repository, and critical pages.
  2. Agree review frequency, access boundaries, and approval responsibilities.
  3. Make approved changes and verify the public result.
  4. Record completed work and flag issues requiring separate decisions.

Emergency response, round-the-clock monitoring, malware removal, hosting charges, and guaranteed uptime are not included unless explicitly agreed. Backup and recovery arrangements depend on the actual hosting platform and must be documented rather than assumed.

Prepare your brief

Bring the site address, platform details, a list of people responsible for approvals, and any recurring issues. Share access only through an agreed secure process and identify the pages that matter most to visitors.

If the issues require a larger structural change, review Web Design & Development instead of treating a rebuild as routine maintenance.
