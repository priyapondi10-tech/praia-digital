How to choose

Identify the main constraint first. A missing foundation, an unclear explanation, a promotion problem, and an upkeep problem call for different work.

  • The structure is confusing: review Web Design & Development; use SEO & Content to define what each page needs to say.
  • The site is ready for a specific offer: review Digital Marketing and confirm the landing page answers the promise made in the campaign.
  • Information is becoming stale: review Website Care and escalate larger content gaps or structural changes to the appropriate guide.

You do not need to buy or schedule every service together. Start with the highest-priority visitor problem and define a review point before expanding the scope.

Prepare a useful project brief

Bring evidence of the problem and name the people who can approve a solution.

Include your current website, priority audience, desired action, existing assets, constraints, and budget boundaries where relevant. Record who owns content approval, technical access, and release decisions. Never share passwords in a public issue or repository.

These are starter definitions, not a fixed-price offer or a promise of results. Timing, costs, deliverables, and recurring responsibilities need written agreement. Maintainers can follow the source catalogue, content guide, and audit record.
