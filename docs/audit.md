Record the source revision, deployment status, crawl identifier and settings, expected and observed URLs, HTTP statuses, content review criteria, related-service link coverage, and any blocked or missing pages. Distinguish a useful editorial expansion from a broken-link defect. After changes, verify both the deployed revision and the live pages again.
