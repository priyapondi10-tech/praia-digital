# Service catalogue and source map

## Catalogue summary

Four service guides support a connected visitor journey: plan and build a usable site, explain its services, promote a focused offer, and maintain the result. These are starter definitions requiring owner confirmation of factual scope and availability. The [hub](https://priyapondi10-tech.github.io/praia-digital/services/) helps visitors choose; the [content guide](content-guide.md) governs future edits.

## Web Design & Development

**Summary:** turn an agreed page map and approved content into responsive pages with review criteria and a practical handover.

- [Live guide](https://priyapondi10-tech.github.io/praia-digital/services/web-design-development/) · [HTML source](../services/web-design-development/index.html)
- Related: [SEO & Content](https://priyapondi10-tech.github.io/praia-digital/services/seo-content/) clarifies page intent; [Digital Marketing](https://priyapondi10-tech.github.io/praia-digital/services/digital-marketing/) aligns landing pages with campaigns; [Website Care](https://priyapondi10-tech.github.io/praia-digital/services/website-care/) owns recurring post-launch checks.
- Boundaries: integrations, migration, domains, and working backend forms need explicit scope.

## SEO & Content

**Summary:** map audience questions to distinct pages, improve factual explanations, and connect relevant next steps with descriptive links.

- [Live guide](https://priyapondi10-tech.github.io/praia-digital/services/seo-content/) · [HTML source](../services/seo-content/index.html)
- Related: [Web Design & Development](https://priyapondi10-tech.github.io/praia-digital/services/web-design-development/) handles structural restrictions; [Digital Marketing](https://priyapondi10-tech.github.io/praia-digital/services/digital-marketing/) connects campaign messages to useful content; [Website Care](https://priyapondi10-tech.github.io/praia-digital/services/website-care/) schedules factual and link reviews.

## Navigation and source maintenance

**Summary:** keep every route discoverable in the visible menus, contextual body links, XML sitemap, and documentation at the same time.

The [home source](../index.html) introduces all four services. The [hub source](../services/index.html) compares starting points. Every public content page has primary navigation plus visible service shortcuts; individual guides also have breadcrumbs and section navigation. Contextual related-service lists include all three complementary services with a reason to follow each link. Menu links do not substitute for those explanations.

The [sitemap](../sitemap.xml) contains exactly six canonical content URLs. Update it when adding or retiring a public page. See the [audit record](audit.md) for measured coverage and the [documentation index](README.md) for the other maintenance files.
